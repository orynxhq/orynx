        Questions

        Is Orynx free to use?

        Yes. Everyone gets free launch kits every day. Once $ORYNX launches, holding 1,000,000 $ORYNX unlocks 10 kits a day and every logo style.

        Can I make a meme coin with it?

        Yes. Switch to Meme coin and Orynx builds a mascot, lore, name and posts made to spread. AI projects get a clean product kit instead.

        Do I own what Orynx makes?

        Yes, use the names, logos and text however you like. Check that a name or ticker isn't already taken on pons and X before you launch.

        Which chain does it support?

        Orynx is built for Robinhood Chain, and kits are ready to use on pons.

        What can't it make?

        Orynx only makes original tokens. It skips ideas that copy existing brands, famous characters or real people, so your launch is yours alone.

        How do I get more kits per day?

        Hold 1,000,000 $ORYNX once it launches, then tap Connect wallet. Signing in is free and never moves your funds.
